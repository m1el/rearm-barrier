**`foo2.rs` is the correct one.** `foo2_nw.rs` is wrong: it sizes the ticket array with `WORKERS` entries, but the completion tree needs `ticket_storage(WORKERS, CLUSTER)` entries, which is often larger. For 2-way clusters that happens at 1, 5, 9 through 13, 17 through 21 and so on. The test in `foo2_nw.rs` only passes because 7 workers with cluster 2 happens to need exactly 7 tickets.

I verified this by changing the test to 5 workers:

```

foo2.rs:     test result: ok
```
